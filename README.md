# Freelance Web Development Pricing Guide

## Introduction

Welcome to the comprehensive pricing guide for freelance web development services. This guide is designed to provide a clear, structured overview of costs associated with building and maintaining various types of websites. 

> **Important Note:** All costs mentioned in this document are **approximate estimates**. The final price can be **extended** for highly complex, feature-rich requirements, or **minimized** for simpler projects depending on your specific website requirements.

## 3. Domain & Hosting Charges (Resource Charges)

These are usually third-party costs and are required to keep the website live on the internet. 
> **Note:** All resource charges listed below are **approximate** and are subject to change based on current market rates and service providers.

| Item | Price | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **.com Domain** | ₹900–₹1,500/year | Paid by client |
| **.in Domain** | ₹600–₹1,000/year | Paid by client |
| **Shared Hosting** | ₹2,000–₹5,000/year | Paid by client |
| **VPS Hosting** | ₹8,000+/year | If required (for high traffic) |
| **Dedicated Server** | ₹25,000+/year | For enterprise/large scale |
| **SSL Certificate** | Free | Let's Encrypt |
